# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want RelayAgent to be a place where anyone can propose an idea, be wrong in public, and get a
straight answer. Everyone participating in this project is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Review the patch, not the person.
- Assume the other person is reasonable and short on context, not stupid or hostile.
- Disagree with the argument. "That breaks fail-loud because X" is a review. "This is garbage" is not.
- Accept a decision once it is made, or take it to a new issue with new evidence.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, or private messages about this project
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Discrimination or demeaning comments based on identity of any sort
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, PRs, discussions, commits) and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to **haemin@musibe.com**. Reports stay confidential. Maintainers will
respond, and may edit, hide, or remove contributions, or block accounts, for behavior they judge
to violate this document. Maintainers who do not follow it face the same consequences.
